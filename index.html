paid social

Filmadí · Instagram A/B test · Oct 2026

Objective: Filmadí, a networking app for film-industry professionals, ran its first paid app-install campaign on Instagram. The question: which video wins installs, a UGC-style video (A) or a cinematic one (B)?

Process: Built a Meta A/B test with the budget split evenly, the same audience and the same ad copy, so the video was the only difference. When delivery stalled, broadened the audience from about 2M to 23–28M. Afterwards, compared what the ads reported with the app’s total installs to check what the ads were really doing.

Results: No winner yet, and the reason is the finding. Meta put 98% of the spend behind the cinematic video, so the two were never compared fairly. App installs reached 25 in the ad week, 2.5× a normal week, but Meta credited only one of them to the ads.

What it means

No winner yet.

The UGC video got only 31 impressions, so the two videos were never compared fairly.

The budget was too small for installs.

Meta learns from about 50 results a week, and this test got one. Its own rule of thumb pointed to a daily budget more than 70 times what each version had.

Clicks are a workable signal.

The cinematic video earned clicks at less than a twentieth of the cost of an install, so a click-optimised test can produce a real answer.

05

brand strategy & retention

Owala · Graduate Capstone · 2026

Objective: Owala's durability was the problem. A bottle that lasts means customers rarely need to repurchase, so the trust the product earns undermines repeat revenue. Growth instead came from limited drops and viral trends, which produced unpredictable spikes and left the brand exposed to trend fatigue. The brief: build consistency, not just demand.

Process: Social listening in Communalytic, search and paid mix analysis, audience profiles built from category and age data. From that we designed Owala Vitality, a daily wellness system with a bundle-and-habit DTC model, mapped to a lifecycle funnel with behavioural KPIs.

Results: A go-to-market strategy where success is measured as behaviour instead of volume: 30-45 day reorder rate, 4x ROAS, lifetime value from $38 to $350+.
